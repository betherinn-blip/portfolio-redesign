The brief

In November 2019 Milk Bar opened its New York flagship inside the Ace Hotel, and it was meant to feel like walking into the bakery itself. I designed the in-store marketing from top to bottom: signs, menus, floor decals, tray liners and all the little things you only notice on your second visit.

I wanted every surface to say something, even the floor. The neon hallway from the Ace Hotel lobby turned into one of the most photographed spots in the store.
