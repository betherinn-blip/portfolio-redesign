Common Thread Closet · Founder

Share more. Buy less.

Common Thread Closet is a clothing-sharing app where friends, families, coworkers and other trusted circles start private, invite-only closets. Members list pieces they own, borrow what they need and lend what they’re not wearing, all among people they know.

What began as a passion project became a real answer to overconsumption and rising costs. Sharing saves people money, keeps clothes and household items in use and builds community wealth. Making sharing a habit keeps every piece in use longer and out of landfills.

As founder, I currently run every part of the business. I launched and ran a members-only beta site, then designed and built the app based on what members told me. I’ve shaped the brand voice, written the community agreements, emails and grant applications, and formed Common Thread Closet LLC in November 2026.

If you’re interested in learning more or joining the mission, reach out at hello@commonthreadcloset.com.
